What it does.

Arginine and citrulline are amino acids your body uses to make nitric oxide, which helps blood vessels relax. That's the whole idea. No herbs you can't pronounce, no proprietary blend.

Who it's for: Men who want a simple daily blood-flow and circulation supplement and like knowing exactly what's in it.

How to take it.
