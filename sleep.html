What it does.

Magnesium helps your muscles and nervous system do their normal job of winding down. Most adults don't get enough from food. Magzen gives you 250 mg from five forms in one capsule.

Who it's for: Adults who want a non-habit-forming, melatonin-free part of their evening routine.

How to take it.

1 capsule in the evening with water, about an hour before bed.

Before you start.
  • Talk to your doctor first if you have kidney disease or take antibiotics, bisphosphonates or other prescription drugs. Magnesium can change how some are absorbed.
  • Higher magnesium intake can loosen stools. If it does, take it with food.
  • Not for use under 18. Ongoing trouble sleeping is worth a conversation with a doctor.
