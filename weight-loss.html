Is it right for you?

Two of the three products can lower blood sugar. Tick anything that applies before you order.

Nothing here flags a problem. Still read every label, and stop if anything feels off.

I take medication for diabetes or blood sugar. Don't start without your doctor. BurnFast (berberine) and GlucoCare (chromium, alpha-lipoic acid) can both lower blood sugar, and together with your medication that can go too low.

I take any other prescription drug. Berberine changes how the liver handles several drugs. Show your doctor or pharmacist the labels first.

I'm pregnant, nursing or trying to conceive. The Weight Stack isn't for you right now. Berberine isn't considered safe in pregnancy.

I'm sensitive to caffeine or have a heart condition. Check whether the final BurnFast formula contains caffeine before starting, and talk to your doctor.

I'm under 18. Not for you. These products are for adults only.
