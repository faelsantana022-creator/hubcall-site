Straight answers.

Is this a prescription or a drug?

No. These are dietary supplements. They're not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and they're not a replacement for anything your doctor prescribed.

How do I take them?

Follow the directions on each label. Most products are one capsule a day; BurnFast is two capsules and KetoSlim is a dropper. Same time every day works best.

Who shouldn't take them?

Anyone under 18. If you take medication (especially for blood pressure, heart conditions or erectile function) or have a medical condition, talk to your doctor first.

When will I be charged?

Today, when you start, and again each time your supply runs out: every 30, 60, 90 or 120 days, depending on the plan you pick. Each renewal is the same plan at the same price. We email you a few days before each charge.

How do I cancel?

Email contato@hubcallsolutions.com from the address you ordered with. No order number or reason needed. We cancel within 1 business day and confirm by email; if you write before your charge date, you won't be charged. You keep the box you already received.

Can I pause or skip a month?

Not yet. You can cancel before any charge and restart later.

Can I buy just one product?

Yes. Every product is sold on its own: $69.90 for one month, $119.90 for two or $149.90 for three. The Men's Stack and the Weight Stack bundle three products and cost less per bottle.

Do you have lab reports?

For some products, yes. The Lab reports page lists every product with a certificate of analysis on file. Email us and we'll send the one for your lot.

Will I feel a difference?

We won't promise you will. Supplements aren't drugs, and bodies differ. What we promise is the exact dose of everything on the label, and an easy exit if it's not for you.
